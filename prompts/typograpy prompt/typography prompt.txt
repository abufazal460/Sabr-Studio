STRICT TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM — DO NOT DEVIATE

You are implementing typography for a premium minimalist interior-design website.

The website uses a BLACK + WHITE visual language with generous whitespace, large editorial headings, clean body typography, high-quality imagery, minimal UI, and a sophisticated architectural/interior-design aesthetic.

TYPOGRAPHY IS A FIXED DESIGN SYSTEM.

DO NOT introduce new font families.
DO NOT replace the selected fonts.
DO NOT randomly change font weights.
DO NOT use arbitrary font styles.
DO NOT create additional typography systems.
DO NOT use decorative fonts.
DO NOT use system fonts unless a fallback is technically required.
DO NOT use different fonts for individual sections.
DO NOT use typography inconsistently between pages.

The ENTIRE WEBSITE must follow the exact typography rules below.
